top recommendation

Give persistence one form

Not because it is the most elegant, but because it is the only candidate where the interface is currently lying. The Windows service is registered with no arguments at all — clap rejects it on first start — and the Unix unit starts the worm without its key, so the re-launched agent reports its loot in cleartext. The persistence modules are shallow in the strict sense: their interface (a name and a path) is nearly as complex as their implementation (a rendered unit), and what they hide is not complexity but an omission.

Candidate 2 is the runner-up and the better structural investment: six call sites hand-assemble the same upload, and the rule that decides cleartext versus sealed lives in the server instead of the type. Fix candidate 1's two defects first — they are bugs, and they are cheap.
